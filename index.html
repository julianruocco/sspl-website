Who We Are

Pioneering space systems engineering through hands-on student-led flight programs and interdisciplinary research.

Our Mission

Founded in August 2006, the Student Space Programs Laboratory (SSPL) coordinates Penn State student space projects and exposes members to the end-to-end systems engineering process.

SSPL provides an interdisciplinary community where students apply classroom theory to flight hardware, sounding rockets, high-altitude balloons, and lunar infrastructure.

Recent Missions

Hands-on space systems engineering across NASA competitions and flight hardware development.

NASA BIG IDEA CHALLENGE 2023

SMELT – Lunar Metal Processing - Innovation Award

Microwave-assisted lunar regolith processing system designed to extract and smelt metals on the Moon's surface for sustainable lunar exploration.

NATIONWIDE ECLIPSE BALLOONING

NEBP Payload & Atmospheric Science

Atmospheric gravity wave research and real-time video telemetry payloads launched during solar eclipse events under NASA sponsorship.

NASA LUNARECYCLE FINALISTS

Space Waste Reclamation

Automated modular recycling of plastic and metallic waste for lunar missions using microwaves to generate cast metal parts, fuel precursors, and char.

Get Involved

Start your space engineering journey with SSPL's Student Training Program (STP).

Student Training Program (STP)

Initiated every January and September, STP takes new students through a complete rapid design-build-launch rocket payload cycle in a single semester.

Gain end-to-end experience from conceptual design, testing, integration, and flight operations to post-flight data analysis.
